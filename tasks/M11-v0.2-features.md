## T38 — Pre-call briefs

**What:** Before a call with a known contact/company, generate a one-page brief: what they told us (top accepted highlights), open follow-ups ☆, open hypotheses needing evidence from this segment, statements to re-verify (drift candidates), and three suggested past-behavior questions. The single highest-frequency-of-use feature in the app.
